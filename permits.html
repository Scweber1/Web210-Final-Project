Permits in Macon County

Guiding Development and Ensuring Safety

Obtaining Permits

Macon County requires permits for various construction, development, and activities to ensure compliance with regulations and promote safety. Here's a guide to help you through the process:

Building Permits

Required for the construction, alteration, or addition to any building or structure.

Apply for a building permit through the Planning and Development Department.

Building Permit Application

Land Use Permits

May be required for certain land development activities, including subdivision, zoning changes, or site plans.

Contact the Planning and Development Department for requirements.

Land Use Regulations

Right-of-Way Permits

Required for any work within the county's right-of-way, such as utility installations or driveway construction.

Obtain right-of-way permits from the Public Works Department.

Right-of-Way Permit Information

Other Permits

Depending on your project, you may also need:

  • Electrical Permits
  • Plumbing Permits
  • Fire Permits

Contact the relevant department for details and applications.
